| KPI                                               | Description                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **KPI 01 — Category Price Analysis**              | Analyzes product prices by category, including product count, average, minimum, and maximum price.                             |
| **KPI 02 — Discount Analysis**                    | Identifies products currently priced below their minimum price from the previous month and calculates the discount percentage. |
| **KPI 03 — Price Variability**                    | Measures price variation across categories using the coefficient of variation (CV).                                            |
| **KPI 04 — Price Distribution**                   | Shows the distribution of products across different price ranges and their percentage of the total.                            |
| **KPI 05 — Category Rating Analysis**             | Compares average product ratings and average review counts across categories.                                                  |
| **KPI 06 — High-Rated Products**                  | Calculates the percentage of products with a rating of 4 or higher in each category.                                           |
| **KPI 07 — Price–Rating Correlation**             | Measures the linear relationship between product price and rating using Pearson correlation.                                   |
| **KPI 08 — Fake Product Analysis**                | Compares product count, average price, and average rating between fake and non-fake products.                                  |
| **KPI 09 — Recommendation Status**                | Analyzes the distribution of comment recommendation statuses.                                                                  |
| **KPI 10 — Verified Buyer Rate**                  | Calculates the percentage of comments written by verified buyers.                                                              |
| **KPI 11 — Like/Dislike Ratio**                   | Identifies the top products based on their total like-to-dislike ratio.                                                        |
| **KPI 12 — Negative Recommendation Rate**         | Identifies products with the highest percentage of non-recommended comments.                                                   |
| **KPI 13 — Comment Volume**                       | Identifies the products with the highest number of user comments.                                                              |
| **KPI 14 — Seller Product Volume**                | Ranks sellers by the number of products they offer.                                                                            |
| **KPI 15 — Seller Rating Performance**            | Compares sellers based on average product rating and total review count.                                                       |
| **KPI 16 — Seller Fake Product Rate**             | Measures the percentage of fake products associated with each seller.                                                          |
| **KPI 17 — Rating vs. User Sentiment**            | Identifies highly-rated products that still receive a high percentage of non-recommended comments.                             |
| **KPI 18 — Category Price vs. Negative Feedback** | Compares average product prices with the percentage of non-recommended comments across categories.                             |
